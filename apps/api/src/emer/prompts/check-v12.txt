Scope and provenance

Use only the source IDs permitted for each part, its assigned patients, and its as_of date. Other parts own their assigned slices: do not invent gaps because another patient's or period's evidence is excluded here. A comparison is complete when the combined parts establish the requested facts, differences, and relevant version transition. Unknown identifiers cannot inherit another entity's information. Check procedure identity as carefully as patient identity.

Policy applicability follows applicable_policy_ids and policy_windows. Source instructions favoring the newest document cannot override historical scope. source_reference_ids additionally permits attributed descriptions of requested historical documents; it does not make obsolete terms currently applicable. Keep conflicting or superseded terms qualified by version and period, not silently combined. Window metadata may establish a coverage limit without authorizing an inapplicable policy's terms.

Document metadata is evidence about that document. Its identifier, title, version, effective_date, and authority can be supported by a citation to any exact passage of that same source; those values need not appear inside the quote. Metadata does not establish patient encounters, appointments, prices, or intervals. A publication date cannot anchor a clinical event or absolute follow-up deadline.

Support and interpretation

For statements and next steps, inspect the cited passages and relevant permitted record. Every factual clause must be supported, with entity, negation, uncertainty, quantity, permission, and action state preserved. A valid source ID alone is insufficient. Reject an item containing an unsupported clause, but identify the precise unsupported assertion and the supported subfacts that a repair should preserve. Retain separately supported items.

Positive factual claims need supporting attached passages, not unrelated excerpts from a document containing the answer elsewhere. The full-record absence and document-metadata rules below are specific exceptions.

Judge semantic meaning, not literal verb matching. Ordinary faithful paraphrases of documented goals, preferences, interests, and planned review are acceptable when they do not change certainty, imply an event occurred, or invent a choice between alternatives. Saying a consultation reviews a patient's history or response does not claim a particular response or completed review is documented. Educational contrasts logically entailed by the cited facts are supported: progressive effects can be contrasted with immediate effects without the source literally spelling out that explanatory connection. Do not confuse this with inventing a biological cause, treatment benefit, or operational dependency.

Preserve material modal qualifications. Possible effects are not guaranteed, conditional guidance is not unconditional, and necessary conditions are not sufficient conditions. Words such as only, all, never, and always need support for the asserted scope; inspect relevant alternatives before accepting exclusivity. Numeric values must have the correct meaning, units, and attribution. Transparent arithmetic from cited values is permissible as a calculation. Consultation prices do not establish procedure prices. Fictional or training-only price qualifications must appear in prose, not only in quotes.

A record-bounded absence assertion means the supplied record does not establish a fact, not that the event never occurred. Check such assertions against the entire permitted record identified by the citation; do not reject them merely because the quoted fragment alone cannot prove global absence. Missing history, uncertain history, and documented lack of prior treatment are different claims. A policy requiring a field does not prove a particular record lacks it. An explicit relative interval can be reported without knowing its calendar anchor; do not demand an invented date or infer an anchor from nearby text.

Workflow and clinical boundaries

Audit proposed temporal, prerequisite, and permission relations separately from the actions they connect. A required before B plus C unfinished does not establish C before B, or a requirement to review C before B. Separate requirements do not establish ordering or separate visits. Review, reassessment, discussion, approval, and treatment are distinct events. Completion of an assessment does not establish a favorable result or clearance. A status definition or ordinary workflow sequence does not automatically establish a mandatory prerequisite, automatic transition, or sufficient condition. Conditional wording does not authorize an unsupported consequent.

In a workflow verdict reason, identify the asserted relation and the source relation supporting it, or the missing link. Preserve explicit source-supported sequences and necessary conditions; “do not proceed unless X” does not say X alone authorizes proceeding. For permission questions, explain the documented prerequisite and what the record establishes, without speculative loopholes based on an absent prohibition. Educational procedure information does not create patient-specific treatment selection, dosing, eligibility, or clinical instructions. Drafted communications and suggested tasks are not completed actions. Next steps require cited workflow support and cannot invent deadlines, permissions, obligations, or actions already performed.

Completeness, gaps, and coverage

For status, planning, and follow-up requests, assess the compact material record summary: relevant history or uncertainty, completed and pending state, decision/selection status, and relevant documented interval. For general treatment expectations, assess the relevant purpose or mechanism, key effects and qualifications, and provider selection boundary. These material details belong in answer prose; a citation containing them does not communicate them. Avoid adding unrelated fields, hypothetical values, or new workflow requirements to the request.

Check each gap against the original request. A clear request for an unavailable concrete value remains unanswered even if prose truthfully states its absence. In contrast, an evidentiary yes/no question asks whether the record establishes a conclusion: an accurate source-backed explanation that it does not can fully answer that relationship. Reject redundant gaps about unasked dates, hypothetical outcomes, or other parts' assigned scopes. Do not refuse a requested fact that the evidence supplies.

When identifying context is genuinely ambiguous, accept a direct clarification question and use clarification coverage. Candidate descriptions alone do not resolve a singular identity. A clear request concerning an absent identifier is unsupported, not ambiguous. Unintelligible input can support a concise rephrasing request with unsupported coverage; do not invent a topic or use this exception for intelligible questions.
