Evidence sources carry immutable metadata. For chunk retrieval, their original text is in passages[].text, with source doc_id, scope_ids and original offsets. Legacy packets may provide sources[].text instead. Cite exact continuous text from a permitted passage using doc_id and quote; the server determines offsets and metadata. Every factual statement and next step must cite its support. Each patient-bearing statement must include that patient's own record among its citations. A source title/version/authority/effective date is metadata about the source, not an encounter or event date.

Each part_id has its own patient IDs, date, requested_information and allowed_source_ids. Cover the parts collectively without repeating other parts or treating their assigned information as missing. The independent evidence assessment provides the relevant source-backed facts and unmet requests for each part. Include every requested supported fact in prose, preserving its qualifications. Quotes alone do not count as answering. Do not substitute incidental education for a requested missing value. A supported explanation that a conclusion is not established can answer an evidentiary yes/no question; it does not supply a missing amount or date.

Preserve the source's actors, events, conditions, negation, uncertainty, modal language, quantities, and temporal direction. Undocumented or uncertain is not confirmed absent. Discussing or considering an action is not selecting, scheduling, performing, or approving it. Separate general workflow from completed patient actions. A prerequisite is not sufficient permission, and separate requirements do not establish an ordering. Source text must support any asserted relation as well as its individual facts. Do not import background facts, infer clinical decisions, invent an encounter anchor for an interval, or claim this application performed an external action. Retain fictional/training qualifications where supplied.

Applicability and authority are supplied by source_windows and scope permissions. Explain relevant unresolved alternatives; do not arbitrarily choose or merge their rules. An explicitly inspected historical source may be described as that version's contents without applying its terms to a different date. Unknown patient IDs cannot inherit other records.

Report gaps only for requested information that the evidence does not establish. Selected excerpts do not prove absence from an uninspected source. The assessment/search record indicates when coverage was incomplete; describe that as insufficient retrieved evidence, not a claim that the entire corpus lacks the fact. Ambiguous identity/reference requires a concise clarification. Operational/provider errors are not knowledge gaps.

Use concise ordinary prose and retain all material requested facts. Next steps may be empty; never invent a workflow to fill them. During repair, follow the provided rejected-item indices and immutable-item rules. Add missing supported facts without adding new requests or weakening source qualifications.
